Supplementary Table 1 | Candidate-clustering search sensitivity at 99%, 97%, 95%, 90%, 75% and 50% sequence identity in 15 CAMPI acquisitions. Each row is one acquisition and arm: the unclustered search and the six MMseqs2/Linclust identity thresholds (80% bidirectional coverage), 105 rows in total. Peptide counts are distinct accepted canonical peptides (peptide q≤0.01, 5–50 residues, I and L merged). Ratios, losses, gains and retained fractions are relative to the unclustered search of the same acquisition. Reference-database recovery is the fraction of peptides accepted in the reference-database search that were also accepted in that arm. KO recovery is the fraction of KEGG Orthology (KO) identifiers compatible with the unclustered accepted peptides that remain compatible with the clustered accepted peptides, using one fixed peptide-to-KO mapping.
